KNOWN LIMITATIONS (by design)
====================================================

- Solana: Burned NFTs (fully closed Orca/Raydium positions) cannot be recovered
- Sui: Destroyed position objects (Cetus/Bluefin/Momentum) cannot be recovered
- HyperEVM APY from DefiLlama is pool-wide (~50%); prjx.com shows position-specific concentrated APR (~129%) — difference is expected for CLMM positions
- ProjectX (PRJX) not indexed on DefiLlama as "projectx" — correct name is "project-x"
- Momentum (Sui) not on DefiLlama — APY always 0%
- Aerodrome: positions are staked in gauges, not held as NFTs — complex to track individually
- Est. Daily/Monthly Fees on detail page are pool-APY projections, not position-specific earnings
